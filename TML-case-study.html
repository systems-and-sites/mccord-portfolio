The Homepage

A lengthy multi-page site condensed into a single Vogue-inspired editorial page. Selective, cinematic, and built to drive applications.

  1. Full-bleed video heroCompressed from 128MB to 18MB so it loads fast on mobile.
  2. Featured In and Streaming AcrossPress logo marquee plus the platforms the network airs on.
  3. The LotThe MBS Media Campus studio story, told in three editorial cards.
  4. Hollywood ExperiencesEvent galleries with a shared lightbox, about 100 photos and videos.

  6. Shows carouselColors adapt to each show cover, Apple Music style, with a Full List quick-jump.

THE CHALLENGE

A full rebrand went live, then got reversed the next day.

The client changed direction often. I kept local master files for every page, so rolling a brand out or back across 28 files was a script, not a rebuild. Every live URL got checked after each pass. Nothing went stale.
